Overview

A significant evolution in program design and delivery

Phase 5 transforms how Positive Partnerships delivers evidence-based support to educators, parents, and carers of autistic school-aged students. Developed in collaboration with a variety of communities across Australia, the new model prioritises practical impact, collaborative learning, and flexible engagement.

All workshops move to a streamlined one-day delivery format, shifting away from the Planning Tool narrative toward core ideas and practical strategies in autism education and home-school collaboration.

Learning Model

Three stages of connected learning

Learning is structured across three integrated stages, creating a richer and more sustained experience for every participant.

01

Pre-Learning

Structured online pathways completed before attending workshops — moving well beyond a single introductory module. Role-specific streams ensure relevance for every participant, with micro-modules, interactive exercises, and planning templates.

Early Career Educators Teaching Assistants School Leadership Experienced Educators Parents & Carers
02

Workshop

One-day, collaborative sessions focused on content best engaged with alongside others. A deliberate shift from slide-based delivery toward discussion, strategy practice, and contextual exploration of autism concepts — shaped by the unique contexts participants bring.

03

Post-Learning

Extended tools, deeper content, and communities of practice focused on implementation support. New modes of online engagement and curation ensure learning continues long after the workshop day.

Workshop Content

Core themes across all services

Content will be authored in collaboration with Autism CRC, with a consistent structure across all Service delivery types, with key strategic differences for each audience. The following are some suggested content focal points that evolve the educational conversations had across previous phases of the program.

Understanding Autistic Experience

  • Sensory differences
  • Communication styles
  • Cognitive processing differences
  • Strengths and interests

"Shifting from deficit thinking toward a neurodiversity perspective."

Universal Design for Autistic Learners

  • Predictable learning environments
  • Flexible communication options
  • Sensory-aware classrooms
  • Clear structure and expectations

Practical Strategies

  • Visual supports
  • Scaffolding executive functioning
  • Reducing cognitive load
  • Supporting regulation and transitions
  • Working with strengths and interests

Collaborative Planning

  • Working in partnership
  • Implementation planning
  • Home-school communication

Digital-First Resources

From printed workbooks to ongoing digital access

Printed participant workbooks will be discontinued in Phase 5. In their place, participants will access high-quality digital resources that support better accessibility, ongoing access to materials, and environmental sustainability.

Participants can use their own devices to access resources, but the focus during sessions will be on collaborative, small-group interaction — not screens. Digital resources will be available before and after workshops for continual learning.

Building Community

Developing Positive Partnerships as an ongoing community

Phase 5 explores new engagement formats to strengthen the brand, build community, and stay agile to the evolving needs of families and educators in the autism education space.

Podcast & Online Learning

  • New podcast conversations on innovation in autism and education
  • Online learning sessions connected to podcast content
  • Collaboration with key voices in the domain

Annual Online Conference

A yearly Positive Partnerships online conference will bring together parents and carers, educators, and guest speakers — creating a large-scale engagement opportunity that extends well beyond regular workshops.

Whole School Delivery

A new structure for deeper school-level impact

After running a Whole School workshop, the team will return to the school for additional sessions — maximising the value of travel and reducing the need for separate standalone parent workshops.

  • Day 1: Parent workshop for families at the school
  • Day 2: Parent/Carer workshop open to the wider local community
Whole School

Expanded Online Engagement

Short, engaging online learning experiences

Positive Partnerships will develop new formats for online engagement — moving beyond lecture-style webinars toward interactive, topic-based online workshops that mirror the collaborative spirit of face-to-face sessions.
